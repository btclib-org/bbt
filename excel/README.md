# Excel Spreadsheets

Every workbook here is generated by `generate.py`, macro-free: a change is
made to that file, not to a workbook, and `uv run --locked python
excel/generate.py` writes every `.xlsx` it names.

FiniteFields.xlsx has one sheet per prime, F3 through F79, each with its
own `prime` and the tables of opposite, inverse, and square roots for
every element in Fp.

EC* are elliptic curve on real numbers and Fp, each in three sheets: "EC
over R" the curve's own shape and chart, "EC over F" the curve's points
over Fp and their chart, "Finite Field" the multiples of the generator
point and their chart. The naming pattern is EC(a,b)-Fp-G(x,y)-NX.xlsx
where (a,b) are the elliptic curve equation coefficients,
Fp the finite field,
(x,y) the generator point,
and X the cardinality of the EC(Fp) group.
